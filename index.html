Why teams switch

What slows distributors
down today

ISSUE / 01

No delivery intelligence

Routes get coordinated by hand every morning. Priority orders slip through, and there's no reliable trail once a cylinder or drum leaves the depot.

ISSUE / 02

Invisible revenue leakage

Cash memos get captured late or not at all, which opens reconciliation gaps and delays real visibility into what's actually been collected.

ISSUE / 03

Stock blind spots

Portal data and the physical count drift apart. By the time someone notices, the discrepancy is already too old to act on quickly.

ISSUE / 04

Attendance and payroll disputes

Attendance tracked on paper or over the phone leaves no clean record — which means payroll disputes and nothing to point to for compliance.

ISSUE / 05

Complaints with no owner

Customer complaints get logged in a notebook or a phone call and then nowhere else, so nobody's accountable for how long resolution takes.

ISSUE / 06

Delayed P&L visibility

Margins and location performance surface weeks later, once someone manually pulls numbers together from stock, sales, and cash records.
